# Marketplace micro product research

This repository holds the operating playbook and research for a portfolio of small marketplace products. The current focus is finding and validating the first product opportunity before committing to a build.

## Documents

- [Operating playbook](docs/micro-product-marketplace/operating-playbook.md) — readable Markdown transcription of the supplied strategy document, including its scoring weights and operating rules.
- [Original playbook](docs/micro-product-marketplace/references/micro_product_marketplace_operating_playbook.docx) — unchanged source DOCX.
- [Opportunity review from 4 October 2026](docs/micro-product-marketplace/research/2026-10-04-opportunity-review.md) — evidence, competing solutions, uncertainties, and proposed validation work across Atlassian, Shopify, and JetBrains.

## Current position

The first research pass recommends validating a narrow Jira Service Management exporter that includes public comments and excludes internal notes. This is a research recommendation, not an approved product or a commitment to build.

Existing exporters already support this workflow. The open question is whether a simpler, lower-cost product would solve a recurring need for buyers who find those alternatives unsuitable. The proposed next step is three to five conversations with JSM administrators who send ticket reports externally, including a check that a CSV-only version would be useful.

The other primary candidates are DataGrip result snapshots, Shopify shipping-profile reconciliation, and Jira subscription handover audits. Their evidence and reasons to hold are recorded in the opportunity review.

## Using the material

The playbook records the supplied strategy; its original current-position table is preserved as a source snapshot. The dated research note records the subsequent findings. Proposed MVP scope, validation gates, and recommendations remain proposals until accepted.

Marketplace features, prices, and APIs can change. Source links and the research date are included so that material assumptions can be checked before development.
